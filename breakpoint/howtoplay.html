How to Play

Welcome to the game! Here's how you can start playing:

  1. Guess a letter in the word and click "Guess" to submit your guess
    1. If the letter you guessed is in the word, it will be revealed
    2. Otherwise, it will be added to the list of wrong guesses
  2. Use your guesses wisely, since you only have 6 attempts
    1. Each incorrect attempt will reduce your remaining attempts
    2. Each correct guess will not affect your remaining attempts
  3. After guessing the word, or running out of attempts, a description will be provided to help you understand it

That's it! Good luck and have fun playing!
